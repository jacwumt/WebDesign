Intro to Tenkara Fishing

A simple guide to a minimalist style of fly fishing

This page introduces the basics of tenkara fishing what it is and how it differs from traditional fly fishing.

Basic Gear

A tenkara setup is intentionally minimal. The core gear includes:

  • A telescoping tenkara rod
  • A fixed line (level line or furled line)
  • A tippet (thin end line connecting to the fly)
  • Flies (often simple, traditional patterns called kebari)
