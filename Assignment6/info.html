Tenkara Fishing Gear

Everything Needed for a Simple Setup

The Rod

A tenkara rod is long, lightweight, and telescopic. It can collapse to a short length for transportation and extend to around ten or twelve feet for fishing.

Line and Tippet

The fishing line attaches directly to the tip of the rod. A thin section of tippet connects the main line to the fly.

Flies and Landing Net

Traditional tenkara flies are called kebari. A small landing net can also help an angler safely land and release a fish.

Basic Gear Guide

Item Purpose Simple Choice
Rod Casts and controls the line 10 to 13 foot rod
Line Carries the fly Level or furled line
Tippet Connects the line to the fly Light clear tippet
Fly Attracts the fish Kebari or other small fly

Basic Setup

  1. Extend the rod
  2. Attach the line
  3. Add the tippet and fly
  4. Begin fishing
