The equipment

Gear

Rod, line, tippet, and kebari make the fishing setup. A landing net and wading staff are useful additions.

Rod

A long, flexible rod does the casting and helps control the fly. Tenkara rods telescope down for carrying, and the line attaches at the tip instead of passing through guides to a reel.

Line

The main line carries the cast from the rod toward the fly. Its length stays fixed while you fish. I use PVC-coated or furled line because I like how both cast and handle.

Tippet

Tippet is the thin section between the main line and the kebari. It makes the connection near the fly less visible. Choose a strength within your rod manufacturer's recommended limit.

Kebari

The artificial fly at the end of the tippet. Thread and feathers form a small pattern around the hook. Different kebari use different bodies and feather arrangements.

Landing Net

A net helps bring a fish under control at the end of the fight. It also lets you keep the fish in the water while preparing to remove the hook.

Wading Staff

A staff provides another point of contact when moving over uneven streambeds. It can help with balance and checking your next step, but it doesn't make deep or fast water safe.
