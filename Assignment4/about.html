About Tenkara

A Simple Approach to Fishing

Origins of Tenkara

Tenkara developed in the mountain streams of Japan. It was used by anglers who needed a simple and effective way to catch fish without carrying a large amount of equipment.

Why People Enjoy It

Many anglers enjoy tenkara because it is lightweight, portable, and easy to set up. The long rod and fixed line also make it useful for carefully presenting a fly in small streams.
