About the guide

A starting point for getting out.

Missoula Community Life helps residents discover activities beyond the sports that already receive plenty of attention.

Who this is for

This guide is for people who want a few clear ideas for spending time around Missoula. You might be new to town, planning an afternoon with family, or looking for an interest to share with someone else.

The goal is simple: help you find an activity, understand the basics, and reach its official information page.

How activities are selected

The guide includes arts, gaming, outdoor places, history, and local businesses. Listings aim to offer a mix of interests and costs, with short descriptions and practical location information.

Official websites are the starting point for listing details. They are linked on each card so you can check schedules, event fees, registration, and available access information.

About this draft

This is a student project by John Weber for MART341 at the University of Montana. It is not an official city or venue website.

Venue photographs and an approved contact address are still being prepared. For questions about a particular activity, please contact the venue through its official website.
