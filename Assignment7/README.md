# Assignment 7 - Missoula Community Life

John Weber / MART341

## Goal and pages
Help Missoula residents find arts, gaming, history, outdoor places, and local businesses.
- Home: introduction, three featured activities, link to Explore.
- Explore: six starter listings, interest jump links, locations, cost notes, official links.
- About: purpose, selection approach, draft status, photo credit.

## New components
- `.community-intro`: NEW Flexbox image/text introduction. Nested `.intro-copy` stacks text and the call to action. Stacks vertically below 700px.
- `.activity-grid`: NEW responsive CSS Grid. `.activity-card` has a photo slot, heading, description, location, cost and official link. Used on Home and Explore.

## Reused components
Header/navigation, skip link, basic button and footer patterns adapted from Assignment6, with new colors/content and three-page navigation. The original Assignment6 files are unchanged.

## Rough draft limits and next steps
- Six listings now; proposed maximum remains 12.
- Category links jump to a representative card; these are not filters.
- Photo boxes are deliberately labeled placeholders, not photos of the venues.
- Home image is reused from the supplied proposal; replace with client-approved or personal imagery before final delivery. Source: https://www.homes.com/local-guide/missoula-mt/video/sbns2g0v2678m/
- No contact address has been supplied; do not invent one. The About page directs venue questions to official websites.

- Before final delivery: confirm images, contact address, listing selection and access details; test in the user's browsers.
