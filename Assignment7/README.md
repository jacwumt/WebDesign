## Goal and pages
Help Missoula residents find arts, gaming, history, outdoor places, and local businesses.
- Home: introduction, three featured activities, link to Explore.
- Explore: six starter listings, interest jump links, locations, cost notes, official links.
- About: purpose, selection approach, draft status, photo credit.
